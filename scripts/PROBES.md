# scripts/probe-*.mjs — the scratch measurement harnesses

These are **not part of the lab**. They are not typechecked, they emit no committed artifact, their
numbers are not digest-bound, and nothing in the site or the papers reads them. They exist so that
every table in the v2.0 documents can be regenerated by hand.

The lab proper is `npm run lab` and the `*-sim.mjs` / `*-analyze.mjs` pairs beside them, which do
produce committed, digest-pinned artifacts. If a result matters enough to cite in a paper, it
belongs there, not here.

## Two permanent instruments that are NOT probes

Monet's roadmap (MONET.md §3.3a, §3.4a, §6.2) makes two home measurements standing instruments,
and they live beside the probes because they share the probes' harness idioms, not their status:
their numbers are quoted in MONET.md's acceptance records and must stay reproducible.

| script | what it answers |
|---|---|
| `calibration.mjs --version vX.Y` | believed vs realised hit probability, per decile, on the chosen asks; and the licence ladder on every legal ask, split by what the model holds |
| `duplicate-pairs.mjs --a vX.Y --b vX.Z` | the home regression cell: two Monet versions on duplicate deals, paired, with the cell's own SD; `--a-override '{"defuse":0}'` lays style keys over a version for an ablation rung |

Both name the version they measure and refuse a missing one: the registry has no "latest".

## Discipline these probes follow

- **Duplicate deals.** Every seed is played in both orientations, so the deal is never a confound
  ([BOT_LAB.md](../BOT_LAB.md) §5.1).
- **A byte-exact control.** Any probe with an appetite must reproduce the shipped policy exactly at
  appetite zero, and print `0.0000 +/- 0.0000` when it does. If it does not, the arm is confounded
  and its numbers mean nothing. `probe-ab.mjs 200 0` and `probe-licence3.mjs 400 0 lic3 0 0` are the
  two standing control invocations.
- **Held-out banks.** Tuning and confirmation use disjoint seed prefixes, named in the output.

## Harness notes, so the next one does not rediscover them

- `us54Config` is a **const object**, not a factory.
- `newGame(seed, config, startSeat)` — positional, not an options object.
- The acting seat is `st.declareWindow ? st.declareWindow.option : st.turn`, not `st.turn`.
- A book's result is `BookResult.outcome` (`'team0' | 'team1' | 'void'`), not `.team`.
- `legalAsksFromView` lives in `lib/engine/helpers.ts`; `rankAsksWith` and `buildKnowledge` in
  `lib/engine/bots/knowledge.ts`; `SKILL_PRESETS` in `lib/engine/bots/style.ts`, not `roster.ts`.
- Anything over ~2 minutes should be backgrounded; a full nine-style sweep is tens of minutes.
